# Trading Optimization Platform: Advanced Prediction Model (Consensus 2.0)

## Overview
Consensus 2.0 is an advanced strategy selection engine designed to provide more stable and robust trading recommendations. Unlike traditional "Classic" selection (ranking by raw average profit) or "Statistical" selection (ranking by Expected Value), Consensus 2.0 uses an ensemble of multiple time horizons and qualitative factors to find "structural" alpha rather than chasing temporary noise.

## 🏁 Key Selection Logics

| Logic | Methodology | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classic** | Ranks strategies by raw Average Profit per trade. | High-volatility scalp identification. |
| **Statistical** | Ranks strategies by **CWEV** (Confidence-Weighted Expected Value). | Probabilistic edge quantification. |
| **Ensemble 2.0** | **Multi-factor consensus** across 30, 45, and 90-day windows. | Long-term account stability and regime survival. |

## 🛠️ Deep Dive: Consensus 2.0

### 1. Multi-Window Validation
The model calculates performance for every strategy across three independent lookback periods:
- **Short-term** (30 days): Detects immediate momentum.
- **Medium-term** (45 days): Filters for temporary market shifts.
- **Long-term** (90 days): Confirms the structural edge.

A strategy only achieves "Consensus" if it performs consistently across these windows.

### 2. Weighted Factor Scoring
Strategies are scored based on a combination of:
- **Profitability (40%)**: Net average trade.
- **Consistency (30%)**: Sharpe ratio and drawdown profile.
- **Recency (20%)**: Recent 14-day performance slope.
- **Regime Fit (10%)**: Alignment with current VIX (Volatility) regime.

### 3. Stickiness & Persistence
To avoid excessive strategy switching (over-fitting to noise), the model includes a **Persistence Bonus**. If a strategy was selected in the previous period and remains profitable, it receives a "stickiness" boost, favoring structural consistency over frequent re-optimization.

### 4. VIX Regime Adaptation
The engine automatically detects the current market volatility regime:
- **Low VIX (<15)**: Prioritizes high win-rate, lower R/R systems.
- **High VIX (>25)**: Prioritizes high-alpha, outlier-capture systems.

## 📊 UI Integration
The model is integrated into the **Recommendation Matrix** and the **Walk-Forward Validation** tool.
- **Matrix View**: Shows consensus winners for each time slot.
- **ML Predictor Grid**: Provides the next 7 days of forecasted strategy selections.
